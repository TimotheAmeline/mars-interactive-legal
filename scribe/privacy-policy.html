Privacy Policy

Scribe - Search-first Handwriting Launcher

Effective Date: December 12, 2025

Your privacy is non-negotiable. Scribe Launcher is built to function entirely offline, without surveillance, tracking, or data collection.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data externally. All data remains entirely on your device.

We do not:

  • Track you
  • Display ads
  • Use analytics or crash reporting tools
  • Store or share data on any server

2. Local Data Storage

Scribe Launcher stores the following locally only:

  • App preferences and settings
  • Wallpaper and appearance choices
  • Handwriting configuration settings
  • Quick-access app selections

This data can be removed at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its local storage.

3. Permissions and Access

Scribe Launcher may request these optional permissions:

Permission Purpose Required?
Default Launcher To replace your home screen Required
Set Wallpaper To apply custom wallpapers Optional
App Listing Access To display installed apps Required

All permissions are processed on-device only and can be revoked at any time via Android settings.

4. Handwriting Recognition

Scribe uses ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition for handwriting input.

  • Recognition runs entirely on your device
  • No handwriting data is sent to any server
  • Language models are downloaded locally and stored on-device

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All data is stored locally as long as the App is installed
  • Uninstalling Scribe Launcher deletes all associated data
  • No cloud sync or remote recovery exists
  • You can optionally export your settings and data for phone migration or as a backup (you are entirely liable for where and how you store this backup file)

6. Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored externally, there is no data access or deletion request process required. You remain in full control of your data through your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Significant changes will be communicated through app updates.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or concerns: mars_interactive@proton.me

Scribe Launcher respects your privacy — and your attention.
