Privacy Policy

Aurora - Minimalist & Intentional Launcher

Effective Date: October 25, 2025

Your privacy is non-negotiable. Aurora Launcher is built to be entirely local, secure, and free from surveillance.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data externally. All data stays entirely on your device.

We do not:

  • Track you
  • Display ads
  • Use analytics or crash reporting tools
  • Store or share data on any server

2. Local Data Storage

Aurora Launcher stores the following locally:

  • Mood configurations and preferences
  • Wallpaper selections and color themes
  • App usage state (e.g., last mood opened)

This information is only available on your device and can be cleared anytime by uninstalling the App or resetting its data.

3. Permissions and Access

Aurora Launcher may request these optional permissions:

Permission Purpose Required?
Default Launcher To replace your home screen Required
Notification Access To show subtle glow indicators Optional
Widget Creation For adding minimal widgets to moods Optional
Lock Screen Access To enable double-tap to lock Optional
Set Wallpaper To allow per-mood wallpapers Optional

All permissions are processed on-device only and can be revoked at any time in Android Settings.

4. Security

Your moods, settings, and preferences are protected by your device's native Android security. We do not access, store, or transmit your personal information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is kept locally as long as the App is installed. Uninstalling Aurora Launcher deletes all associated data. There is no cloud sync or remote recovery possible.

6. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored externally, there is no need to request access or deletion. You maintain full control over your data through your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app evolution. You'll be notified via app updates if significant changes occur.

8. Contact

Questions or privacy concerns? mars_interactive@proton.me

Aurora Launcher respects your privacy and your attention. Always.
