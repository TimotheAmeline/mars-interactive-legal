Maintain a healthy social life and meaningful connections. Memo amplifies your social life and offloads your brain by storing, organizing, and tracking information.

Your friend is chronically 15 minutes late, and you just realized you've forgotten your cousin's birthday for the third year running. You said "yes" to too many meetings and events. You feel drained or you're clearly not seeing enough sunlight...

Life gets messy. Friendships fade. Important conversations get postponed indefinitely. The hatched remains buried while nobody remembers what the grudge was in the first place.

Memo changes that, for good.

Stop relying on your overwhelmed brain to remember every social detail. Transform scattered mental notes into a clear system that helps you show up for the people who matter. Track your social energy, remember what actually matters to each person, and build stronger relationships through intentional connection.

No algorithms deciding who you should talk to. Memo is a power tool that amplify your natural social instincts while offloading your brain by storing, organizing, and making calculations for you. And it's completely free, without any ads or trackers.

Core Features:

  1. Social Insights tracking with useful statistics overall, or per contact. Engage with others better, and navigate healthier relationships.
  2. Contact sentiment history and relationship goal setting. Set challenges for yourself and for each of your contacts to better your social life. Keep improving consciously, don't wait for things to improve out of nowhere: take control of who you meet and how you interact.
  3. Log a ton of information per contact. From meeting preparation reminders, important dates, through a gift registry...
  4. Network visualization and group dynamics mapping. Visualize how your contacts interact with each other, make people meet each other.
  5. Complete contact import and calendar integration. Don't waste time logging meetings or creating contacts one by one.
  6. Smart notifications that respect your boundaries. Notifications can be tailored down to the last minute detail, giving you full control.

Your Privacy, Protected:

  1. Zero tracking.
  2. Zero ads.
  3. Zero data harvesting.
